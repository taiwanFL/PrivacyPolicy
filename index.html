Privacy Policy

Forgehold, Dodgeforge and Sortforge (Android)
Effective date: 1 November 2026

In short: the games have no accounts and send no personal information to us. Your progress and settings stay on your device. The games show ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Who we are

These games are made by TWFL ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Android games Forgehold, Dodgeforge and Sortforge (the "games"), published on Google Play. If you have any question about it, contact us at .

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information. The games have no sign-in, no chat, no analytics service and no servers of our own.

Your game progress and settings (for example your best level, sound, vibration, style and language choices) are saved only in the games' private storage on your device. They are never sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games are free and show ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC: a banner at the top of the screen, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for a bonus (for example extra time). To show and measure ads, the AdMob software in the games collects information from your device and sends it to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (or an app set ID);
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as ad views and taps;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information about the ad software.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you have allowed it, to show personalised ads. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you; we only see total figures such as how many ads were shown.

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open a game, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised. If you decline, you will see non-personalised ads, which are based only on general context such as the game and your approximate location.
  • Personalised ads anywhere: you can reset your advertising ID, or delete it so it can't be used for personalised ads, in your phone's settings (on most phones: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Google's ad settings are at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you never have to watch one to keep playing.
  • Your game data: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android settings, to delete everything the game saved on your device.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share any personal information, because we don't hold any. The only third party that receives data from the games is Google, through AdMob, as described above.

Data retention and deletion

We keep no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data collected by Google is kept and deleted under Google's own policies; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, and to object to its use. Because we don't hold personal information, requests about ad data are best sent to Google using the links above. You are still welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used a game in a way that sent personal information to Google, contact us and we will help you ask Google to remove it.

Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps can't read. Data sent to Google by AdMob is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the games handle information, for example by adding a new service, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the games.
